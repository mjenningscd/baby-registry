Add from any store

A one-tap button for your browser. Open a product on Baby Bunting, Big W, Amazon or anywhere else, tap the button, and the item opens on the registry with its name, price and photo filled in, read straight from the page you're looking at.

On a Mac or PC

  1. Show your bookmarks bar (Safari: View → Show Favourites Bar · Chrome: Cmd+Shift+B).
  2. Drag this button onto the bar:
    + Baby Jennings
  3. On any product page, click + Baby Jennings.

On iPhone (Safari)

  1. Tap Copy the button code below.
  2. Bookmark this page (Share → Add Bookmark), name it + Baby Jennings, save.
  3. Open Bookmarks, tap Edit, tap the new bookmark, delete its address and paste the code. Done.
  4. On a product page, tap the address bar, type Baby and tap + Baby Jennings in the list.

You need to be signed in on the registry (Matt & Grace sign in) for it to add. Check the details, then tap Add.
